### Telephone ringing sound

?Model 500 Telephone British ring? by CianMcCann, [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Model_500_Telephone_British_ring.ogg), licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The original recording is distributed unmodified in `public/sounds/telephone-ring.ogg`.

Additional telephone bell sounds are excerpts, downmixed and re-encoded from
[Automatic telephone Iskra ATA 2 - ringing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Automatic_telephone_Iskra_ATA_2_-_ringing.webm)
and [Telephone 'Nikola Tesla' - ringing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Telephone_%27Nikola_Tesla%27_-_ringing.webm)
by Work With Sounds, licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Telephone pickup and hang-up sounds are excerpts, downmixed and re-encoded from
[Rotary Phone Pick up and Hang up](https://freesound.org/people/cookies%2Bpolicy/sounds/556491/)
by cookies+policy, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
